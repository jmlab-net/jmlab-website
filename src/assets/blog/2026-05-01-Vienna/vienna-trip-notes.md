## Key Themes & Takeaways

1. **Walkability:** Vienna is easy to explore spontaneously; no strict plans needed
2. **Unexpected discoveries:** Light shows, hidden bars, random attractions happen just by walking
3. **Hop-on-hop-off tour:** Best as Day 1 activity to orient yourself, then explore spontaneously
4. **Historic meets modern:** Palace weddings with DJs, classical architecture with contemporary experiences
